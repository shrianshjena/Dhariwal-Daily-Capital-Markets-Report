• Results season: Hindalco and Bosch reported September-quarter profit growth on Friday, and Mahindra & Mahindra led the NIFTY 50 after its results. Company earnings feed into index-level earnings, one of the measures used to assess equity valuations.
• Inflation backdrop: Consumer price inflation was 5.54% in September (provisional), up from 4.83% in August, according to MoSPI data. Inflation shapes the RBI's rate decisions, which reach bond yields and borrowing costs.
