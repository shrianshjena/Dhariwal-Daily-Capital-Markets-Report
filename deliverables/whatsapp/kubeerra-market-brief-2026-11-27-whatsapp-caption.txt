*Market drivers*
• Crude, the rupee and bonds: Brent settled at $96.57 on Thursday, up $1.96 or 2.07% (The Nation), after Iran's Revolutionary Guards said two tankers hit sea mines north of Oman's Khasab port and Yemen's Houthis claimed an attack on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery (Reuters via Euronext). The rupee closed at a provisional 92.44 per dollar (PTI), and the 10-year yield rose 7 basis points as dealers sold bonds on crude (Business Standard). Oil reaches India through the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation.
• Foreign buying: Foreign investors bought a net ₹1,27,945 crore of Indian shares from 1 to 26 November (Kotak Neo; provisional), and Business Upturn counted 18 sessions of net buying in a row. Foreign flows reach index heavyweights and the rupee.
• US tariff proposal: The US Trade Representative's proposed tariffs of up to 25% on Indian goods, or 17% with a trade deal (Business Standard), are open for public comment until 5 Jan 2027, with a hearing on 25 Jan 2027; India has rejected the excess-capacity finding (Business Today). Tariffs reach India through export-oriented sectors and the trade balance.
• Rate expectations: The repo rate is 5.50% after a 25 basis point rise in October, and the next policy meeting runs from 2 to 4 Dec (RBI). Trading Economics reports growing expectations of a further 25 basis point rise on 4 Dec. Policy rates reach bank lending rates, deposit rates and bond yields.
• Growth data: July to September GDP is due on Mon 30 Nov (MoSPI). A Business Standard compilation of 11 agencies' estimates has a median of 7.7%, in a range of 7.2% to 8.1%. Growth figures shape earnings estimates and feed into the RBI's decision.
