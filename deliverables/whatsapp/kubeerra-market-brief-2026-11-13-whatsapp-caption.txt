*Market drivers*
• Inflation and the RBI: October retail inflation rose to 6.09% from 5.54%, with consumer food inflation at 8.94% (Reuters, Business Today). SBI Research now expects a 25 basis point rise at the RBI's 2 to 4 December review rather than 50, and Bank of Baroda's Madan Sabnavis expects 25 or 50 (Business Today). Rate expectations reach bond yields, deposit rates and loan rates.
• Crude oil: Brent settled 3.3% higher at $95.82 on Thursday after Yemen's Houthis claimed attacks on Saudi energy sites, having fallen about $1 in early Asian trade (Reuters). India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation.
• The rupee and bonds: The rupee closed 33 paise weaker at a provisional 94.04 per dollar as the dollar firmed (PTI), and the 10-year G-Sec yield was about 7.25%, up 4 basis points (Trading Economics). A weaker rupee raises the cost of imports; bond prices fall when yields rise.
• Foreign flows and index changes: Foreign investors bought a net ₹73,566 crore in the cash market from 1 to 12 Nov (provisional, Kotak Neo). Separately, Nuvama estimates about $2 billion of passive inflows from MSCI's November index review, effective 30 Nov (Business Standard). Foreign flows feed into large-cap prices and the rupee.
