*At a glance*
• The NIFTY 50 fell 1.75% to 25,349.65 and the Sensex 1.77% to 82,435.99 on Monday 23 Nov; Nifty Bank fell 3.17% to 62,585.35 (Upstox, Liquide, Kotak Neo). Indian markets were shut on Tuesday for Guru Nanak Jayanti and reopen today.

• IBJA's Monday afternoon rate put gold at ₹1,24,945 per 10 g, up 0.85% from Friday; on Tuesday spot gold fell about 2% to about $3,557 an ounce (IBJA, The Nation).

*Market drivers*
• Rate expectations: In a Reuters poll taken 13 to 20 Nov, most of 61 economists expected the RBI to raise the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.75% on 4 Dec; Reuters noted that inflation rose above the upper end of the RBI's 2% to 6% band in October. Policy rates reach bank lending rates, deposit rates and bond yields.
• Foreign buying: Foreign investors bought a net ₹5,673 crore on Monday and ₹1,17,622 crore from 1 to 23 November (Kotak Neo; provisional), while domestic institutions were net sellers. Flows reach large financials, the largest weight in the NIFTY 50, and the rupee.
• Crude and the rupee: Brent settled at $91.40 on Tuesday, up $2.03, after reports of projectiles hitting ships near the Strait of Hormuz (The Nation); the rupee closed at a provisional 92.92 per dollar on Monday (PTI). Oil reaches India through the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation.
• Growth data: Business Standard's survey of 11 agencies puts the median estimate for July to September GDP growth at 7.7% (range 7.2% to 8.1%), against 7.8% in April to June; the data is due on 30 Nov. Growth figures shape earnings estimates and feed into the RBI's decision.
