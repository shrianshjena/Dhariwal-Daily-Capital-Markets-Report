*Kubeerra Market Brief* · Friday 6 Nov 2026

*At a glance*
• The NIFTY 50 rose 2.40% to 24,536.60 and the Sensex 2.60% to 79,310.01 on Thursday, a sixth straight gain and the largest one-day rise in seven months (Upstox News).
• Nearly every sector index rose, led by Nifty Auto (up 4.14%), while media and energy fell (Upstox News, HDFC Sky). Bajaj Finance, up 7.65%, led the NIFTY 50.
• Foreign investors bought a net ₹14,975 crore and domestic institutions sold ₹10,445 crore (provisional). Business Standard said the foreign figure was the highest since February 2021.
• Overnight, the Nasdaq fell 5.52%, its worst day since April 2025, and the S&P 500 4.36% as chip and AI shares were sold; Brent crude settled 3.88% higher at $98.29 after tanker attacks near Hormuz (Reuters).

*Market drivers*
• Foreign flows: Foreign investors bought a net ₹14,975 crore on 5 Nov (provisional), after ₹7,284 crore on 4 Nov. Foreign flows feed into large-cap share prices and the rupee; provisional figures can be revised when final data is published.
• Crude oil: Brent touched an intraday low of about $93.50 a barrel on 5 Nov (Upstox News), then settled 3.88% higher at $98.29 after fresh tanker attacks near the Strait of Hormuz, Reuters reported. India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee and inflation.
• The rupee and bonds: PTI said lower crude and foreign inflows lifted the rupee to 94.41 per dollar. The 10-year government bond yield fell to 7.17%, down 24 basis points so far in November, Business Standard reported. Bond prices rise when yields fall.
• Inflation: Retail inflation was 5.54% in September, up from 4.83% in August (Business Today). October's reading is due on 12 Nov, according to MoSPI's release calendar.
