*At a glance*
• The NIFTY 50 rose 0.63% to 23,960.60 and the Sensex 0.50% to 77,301.40 on Wednesday, a fifth straight gain. Smaller companies did better: the NIFTY Smallcap 100 rose 2.00% and the Midcap 100 1.55%.

• Brent crude settled 8.7% lower at $94.62 a barrel, its lowest since 31 August, as the US midterm results raised bets on an end to the Iran war (Reuters). On Wall Street the Dow rose 3.03%.

*Market drivers*
• Crude oil: Brent settled at $94.62 a barrel on 4 Nov, down 8.7% in a day, as investors weighed whether the US midterm results bring an earlier end to the Iran war, Reuters reported. India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee, fuel costs and inflation.
• Foreign flows: Foreign institutions bought a net ₹7,284 crore on 4 Nov (provisional), after ₹6,837 crore on 3 Nov. Foreign flows feed into large-cap share prices and the rupee; provisional figures can be revised when final data is published.
• The rupee: The rupee rose 17 paise to 94.58 per dollar (PTI, provisional). Reuters reported a two-week high early in the day, with gains trimmed by importer dollar demand, and named a weaker dollar and lower oil after the midterms as the drivers.
• IT shares: Nifty IT fell 1.50% on a day the wider market rose, with TCS and HCL Technologies the largest NIFTY 50 decliners. Trading Economics attributed the falls to profit-taking amid concerns about global technology demand.
