*Market drivers*
• Inflation in India: October retail inflation is due today. ICRA's Aditi Nayar expects 6.1% (NewsBytes) and Bank of Baroda's economists about 6%, citing food prices (Devdiscourse); September's reading was 5.54%. Inflation readings shape rate expectations, which reach bond yields and loan rates.
• Foreign flows: Foreign investors bought a net ₹65,735 crore in the cash market from 1 to 11 Nov while domestic institutions sold ₹40,504 crore (provisional, Kotak Neo). Foreign flows feed into large-cap share prices and the rupee; provisional figures can be revised.
• Crude oil: Brent settled at $92.76 on Wednesday, up $1.27, after Iran said its enriched nuclear material would stay in the country (Reuters); it had fallen to a two-month low on Tuesday. India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation.
• The rupee and bonds: The rupee firmed 6 paise to 93.71 on Wednesday on foreign inflows and crude near a two-month low during Indian trading (PTI); Brent rose later in the day. The 10-year yield rose 6 basis points to 7.21% as US Treasury yields climbed, with a ₹34,000 crore government bond auction due on Friday (Business Standard, Reuters). Bond prices fall when yields rise.
