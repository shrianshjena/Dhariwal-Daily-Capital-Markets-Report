*Market drivers*
• Inflation and the RBI: October wholesale price inflation rose to 12.29% from 11.39%, with fuel and power at 32.84% (Upstox News). Barclays expects a 25 basis point repo rate rise on 4 December and another in February, to 6%; economists at Emkay Global and IDFC First Bank also expect rises at both meetings, from a repo rate of 5.50% (Business Standard). Rate expectations reach bond yields, deposit rates and loan rates.
• Crude oil: Brent's January contract settled 3.55% lower at $92.44 a barrel on Monday. Bloomberg cited ample physical supply, with the IEA expecting a surplus of about 2 million barrels a day this quarter, even as attacks on tankers near the Strait of Hormuz continued (Rigzone). India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation.
• Foreign flows and the rupee: Exchange data show foreign investors bought a net ₹84,167 crore of Indian shares from 1 to 16 November (provisional, Kotak Neo). HDFC Sky, citing NSDL data, said November's buying so far, about $8.9 billion, is already the largest for any month on record, and linked it partly to worries about AI-linked trades overseas. PTI said the inflows helped the rupee gain 27 paise on Monday.
