*Kubeerra Market Brief* · Friday 20 Nov 2026

*At a glance*
• The NIFTY 50 rose 1.14% to 25,514.30, its first close above 25,500 since February, and the Sensex 1.21% to 83,021.75 on Thursday; Nifty Bank rose 1.65% to a record 63,063 (Business Standard, Liquide).
• Nifty Auto rose 4.40%, led by Maruti Suzuki, which Liquide linked to easing copper and aluminium prices; Nifty IT fell 1.10%, the only sector index lower. The NIFTY Midcap 100 rose 1.06% and the Smallcap 100 0.71% (DSIJ, Business Standard).
• Foreign investors bought a net ₹7,411 crore and domestic institutions sold a net ₹1,357 crore (provisional, Kotak Neo). The rupee rose 9 paise to 93.32 per dollar, a fourth straight gain (PTI).
• Overseas, the Dow fell 2.19%, the S&P 500 2.02% and the Nasdaq 2.25% on Thursday, as chip shares fell and Anthropic's first day of trading did not lift other AI stocks (Reuters).
• IBJA had not published Thursday's rates when this issue was prepared; its latest, for Wednesday afternoon, put gold at ₹1,31,164 per 10 g. Spot gold fell about 1.5% on Thursday (IBJA, Reuters).

*Market drivers*
• Foreign buying: Foreign investors bought a net ₹7,411 crore on Thursday after ₹5,867 crore on Wednesday, and ₹1,01,641 crore from 1 to 19 November (provisional, Kotak Neo); HDFC Sky put November's foreign buying at a record $11.28 billion. Flows reach large-cap demand and the rupee.
• Input costs: Kotak Neo linked Thursday's gains in auto shares to softer raw material prices, and in FMCG shares to expected relief on input costs; Upstox linked the rise in air-conditioner makers to copper at a seven-month low. Input prices reach company margins.
• Crude and the rupee: Brent settled at $91.98 on Thursday (Bloomberg), and HDFC Sky said it had fallen about 21.6% in November. The rupee closed at 93.32 per dollar; Anuj Choudhary of Mirae Asset Sharekhan expects it to trade between 92.95 and 93.50 (PTI). Oil reaches India through the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation.
