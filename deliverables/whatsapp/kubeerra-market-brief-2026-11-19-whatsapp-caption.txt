*At a glance*
• The NIFTY 50 fell 0.63% to 25,227.95 and the Sensex 0.61% to 82,031.81 on Wednesday as investors booked profits after November's gains; Nifty Bank fell 0.38% to 62,038.65 (HDFC Sky, Marketcalls).

• IBJA's afternoon rates on Wednesday put gold at ₹1,31,164 per 10 g and silver at ₹1,63,733 per kg, down ₹3,037 and ₹5,438 on the day (IBJA).

*Market drivers*
• Rate expectations in India: Goldman Sachs projects a 25 basis point rise in the RBI's repo rate at the December meeting and a further 50 basis points in the first half of 2027, taking it from 5.50% to 6.25%; SBI Research also expects a 25 basis point rise in December (Business Standard, IANS). Rate expectations reach bond yields, deposit rates and loan rates.
• Wholesale inflation: Wholesale price inflation rose to 12.29% in October from 11.39% in September, with fuel and power at 32.84%, according to provisional data released on 16 Nov; the government cited mineral oils, crude petroleum, natural gas and electricity (Kotak Neo). Input costs reach company margins and, over time, retail prices.
• Crude, flows and the rupee: Brent settled 1.28% lower at $91.95 on Wednesday after US crude stocks rose 3.4 million barrels in the week to 13 Nov (Reuters, The Nation). Foreign investors bought a net ₹94,229 crore of Indian shares from 1 to 18 November (provisional, Kotak Neo). Oil reaches India through the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation; flows reach large-cap demand and the currency.
