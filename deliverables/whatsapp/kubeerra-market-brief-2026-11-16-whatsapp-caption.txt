*Market drivers*
• Inflation and the RBI: October retail inflation rose to 6.09% from 5.54%, above the RBI's 6% upper tolerance limit, with food inflation at 8.94% (PTI, Outlook Business). Radhika Rao of DBS Bank expects another rate rise in December, and ICRA's Aditi Nayar said a 25 basis point rise at the 2 to 4 December meeting appears almost certain (Outlook Business). Rate expectations reach bond yields, deposit rates and loan rates.
• Wholesale prices: Wholesale price inflation was 11.39% in September, up from 9.92% in August, with fuel and power at 25.85% (DD India); the October figure is due today. Wholesale prices feed into companies' input costs and, with a lag, into consumer prices.
• Crude oil: Brent settled at $95.84 on Friday after a second weekly fall. Bloomberg pointed to more Middle East supply and an International Energy Agency forecast of a surplus this quarter, against continued attacks on shipping in the Gulf region (Rigzone). India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation.
• Foreign flows: Exchange data show foreign investors bought a net ₹79,302 crore of Indian shares from 1 to 13 November (provisional, Kotak Neo). Geojit's VK Vijayakumar linked the buying to money moving away from AI-linked markets (IANS). Foreign buying adds demand for large-cap shares and for rupees.
