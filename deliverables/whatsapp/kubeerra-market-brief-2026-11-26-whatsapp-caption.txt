*Market drivers*
• Foreign buying: Foreign investors bought a net ₹1,22,101 crore of Indian shares from 1 to 25 November (Kotak Neo; provisional); PTI, citing NSDL data to 20 Nov, called November's inflow the largest on record. Foreign flows reach index heavyweights and the rupee.
• Crude and the rupee: Brent settled at $94.61 on Wednesday, up 3.5%, after the Houthis said they had attacked Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery (The Nation); during Indian hours it had been lower, and the rupee closed at a provisional 92.16 per dollar (PTI). Oil reaches India through the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation.
• US tariff proposal: The US Trade Representative proposed Section 301 tariffs on 14 economies, up to 25% on Indian goods and 17% if a trade deal is concluded; India's Department of Commerce rejected the excess-capacity finding and said the notice is under examination (Business Standard, PTI). Tariffs reach India through export-oriented sectors and the trade balance.
• Rate expectations: In a Reuters poll taken 13 to 20 Nov, 56 of 61 economists expected the RBI to raise the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.75% on 4 Dec. Policy rates reach bank lending rates, deposit rates and bond yields.
• Growth data: July to September GDP is due on 30 Nov; Deccan Herald reports a Finance Ministry nowcast of 7.35%, against 7.8% in April to June. Growth figures shape earnings estimates and feed into the RBI's decision.
