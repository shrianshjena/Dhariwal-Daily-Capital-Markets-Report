*Kubeerra Market Brief* · Monday 23 Nov 2026

*At a glance*
• The NIFTY 50 rose 1.12% to 25,800.60 and the Sensex 1.08% to 83,919.71 on Friday, both nine-month highs; Nifty Bank rose 2.49% to 64,635.20 (HDFC Sky, Business Standard). Over the week the NIFTY 50 gained 2.61% (Business Standard).
• The NIFTY Midcap 100 rose 0.66% and the Smallcap 100 slipped 0.13%; Nifty Media fell 1.68%, the weakest sector (Business Standard).
• Foreign investors bought a net ₹10,308 crore and domestic institutions sold a net ₹4,860 crore (provisional, Kotak Neo). The rupee closed near 92.67 per dollar, a seven-month high (Reuters, HDFC Sky).
• After India closed, the S&P 500 fell 3.70% and the Nasdaq 5.28% on Friday as AI-linked shares were sold; the S&P 500 entered correction territory (investingLive, Investrade).
• IBJA's Friday afternoon rate put gold at ₹1,23,893 per 10 g, down 4.35% from Thursday afternoon; spot gold fell 2.38% to about $3,538 an ounce (IBJA, Kitco).

*Market drivers*
• Foreign buying: Foreign investors bought a net ₹10,308 crore on Friday and ₹1,11,949 crore from 1 to 20 November (provisional, Kotak Neo); Kotak Neo said it was a fourth straight week of foreign buying. Flows reach large financials, the largest weight in the NIFTY 50, and the rupee.
• Crude and the rupee: Reuters reported Brent heading for a third weekly fall as shipments through the Strait of Hormuz returned to roughly pre-war levels, and the rupee closed near 92.67 per dollar, a seven-month high (Reuters). Oil reaches India through the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation.
• Inflation and the RBI: October retail inflation was 6.09%, above the RBI's 6% upper tolerance limit; Vinod Nair of Geojit Investments said markets may start to price in a possible rate rise (Kotak Neo). Policy rates reach bank lending rates and bond yields.
