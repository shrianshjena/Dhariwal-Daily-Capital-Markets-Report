*Market drivers*
• Crude oil: Brent settled at $91.49 on Tuesday, its lowest since 31 Aug, after President Trump said he would put off new strikes on Iran and Middle East shipments neared prewar levels (Reuters, Rigzone). India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation.
• Foreign flows: Foreign investors bought a net ₹53,172 crore in the cash market from 1 to 9 Nov while domestic institutions sold ₹32,069 crore (provisional, Kotak Neo). Foreign flows feed into large-cap share prices and the rupee; provisional figures can be revised.
• Inflation in India: Retail inflation was 5.54% in September (provisional), with food prices up 7.74% (Business Standard). ICRA's Aditi Nayar expects October's reading at 6.1% (NewsBytes). Inflation readings shape rate expectations, which reach bond yields and loan rates.
• The rupee and bonds: The rupee firmed 5 paise on Monday on easing crude and foreign inflows, PTI reported, while the 10-year yield rose to 7.17% as investors took profits after a rally (Trading Economics). Bond prices rise when yields fall.
