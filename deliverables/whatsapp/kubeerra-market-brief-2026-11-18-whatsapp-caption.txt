*Market drivers*
• Inflation and the RBI: October retail inflation rose to 6.09% from 5.54%, above the RBI's 6% upper limit for the first time in two years, with food inflation at 8.94%. ICRA's Aditi Nayar called a further 25 basis point rise in December nearly certain, and DBS Bank and 360 ONE Asset also expect a rise at the 2 to 4 December meeting (Outlook Business). Rate expectations reach bond yields, deposit rates and loan rates.
• Bond yields and crude: India's 10-year government bond yield fell about 7 basis points to 7.27% on Tuesday as crude was lower during Indian trading, easing worries about imported inflation (Trading Economics). Brent later settled 0.76% higher at $93.14; the IEA expects supply to exceed demand by about 2 million barrels a day this quarter (Transport Topics). Oil reaches India through the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation.
• Foreign flows and the rupee: Foreign investors bought a net ₹88,363 crore of Indian shares from 1 to 17 November (provisional, Kotak Neo), and CNBC-TV18 said their buying has run for 12 sessions. PTI said the inflows and softer crude helped the rupee rise 43 paise to 93.51. Flows reach large-cap demand and the currency.
