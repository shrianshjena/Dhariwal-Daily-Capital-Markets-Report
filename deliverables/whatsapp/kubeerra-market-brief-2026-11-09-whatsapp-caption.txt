*Market drivers*
• Crude oil: Brent fell more than 13% last week after the US midterm results, The National reported. Bloomberg put the drop above 16% as investors weighed the chance of US and Iran talks, and cited Rystad Energy's Jorge Leon saying US pressure does not mean a deal is close. India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation.
• Foreign flows: Foreign investors were net buyers of ₹45,960 crore in the cash market from 2 to 6 Nov while domestic institutions sold ₹24,197 crore (provisional, Upstox). Foreign flows feed into large-cap share prices and the rupee; provisional figures can be revised.
• Inflation and the RBI: Eleven economists polled by Financial Express expect October retail inflation at 6.1%, against 5.54% in September; ICRA's Aditi Nayar also expects 6.1%. The RBI raised the repo rate to 5.50% in October (Business Standard). Inflation readings shape rate expectations, which reach bond yields and loan rates.
• The rupee and bonds: The rupee firmed 52 paise on Friday on a softer dollar, lower crude and buoyant shares, PTI reported. The 10-year yield was 7.19% (Business Standard). Bond prices rise when yields fall.
