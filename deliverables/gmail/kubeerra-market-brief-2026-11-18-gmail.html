2.1 · WHAT IS DRIVING MARKETS

Inflation above the RBI's band, softer bond yields and 12 sessions of foreign buying frame the week

Inflation and the RBI. October retail inflation rose to 6.09% from 5.54%, above the RBI's 6% upper limit for the first time in two years, with food inflation at 8.94%. ICRA's Aditi Nayar called a further 25 basis point rise in December nearly certain, and DBS Bank and 360 ONE Asset also expect a rise at the 2 to 4 December meeting (Outlook Business). Rate expectations reach bond yields, deposit rates and loan rates. Outlook Business

Bond yields and crude. India's 10-year government bond yield fell about 7 basis points to 7.27% on Tuesday as crude was lower during Indian trading, easing worries about imported inflation (Trading Economics). Brent later settled 0.76% higher at $93.14; the IEA expects supply to exceed demand by about 2 million barrels a day this quarter (Transport Topics). Oil reaches India through the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation. Trading Economics, Transport Topics, Financial News (Korea)

Foreign flows and the rupee. Foreign investors bought a net ₹88,363 crore of Indian shares from 1 to 17 November (provisional, Kotak Neo), and CNBC-TV18 said their buying has run for 12 sessions. PTI said the inflows and softer crude helped the rupee rise 43 paise to 93.51. Flows reach large-cap demand and the currency. Kotak Neo FII/DII data, CNBC-TV18 via TradingView, PTI via ThePrint

5.4 · MORE NEWS

Other news that matters for Indian investors

POLICY · ON THE RADAR

SEBI is weighing limits on new margin-funded share purchases, Reuters reported

Citing two unnamed sources, Reuters reported that SEBI is considering stopping fresh margin trading facility positions in a stock once margin-funded holdings reach about 5% to 10% of its free float; the limits are not final. The margin trading book stands at ₹1.53 lakh crore, up 29% in a year, with about two-thirds in small and mid caps (Business Today, Kotak Neo). No rule has changed.

WHY IT MATTERS  Margin funding is borrowed money used to buy shares, so any limit would reach leveraged demand for heavily margin-funded small and mid caps.

Sources: Business Today (citing Reuters), Kotak Neo

 

ECONOMY · SECTOR

The government raised export levies on diesel to ₹14 a litre and on jet fuel to ₹12.50 a litre

In its fortnightly review, the Finance Ministry raised the diesel export levy from ₹11.50 to ₹14 a litre and the jet fuel (ATF) export duty from ₹11 to ₹12.50 a litre from 16 Nov; the petrol export levy stays at zero. It is the second rise in three weeks (Daily Jagran, IANS).

WHY IT MATTERS  The levies set how much refiners keep on fuel exports, so they reach the margins of export-oriented refiners.

Sources: Daily Jagran, IANS via Social News XYZ

KNOW YOUR CATEGORIES · OVERNIGHT FUND

Under SEBI's categorisation circular of 26 February 2026, an overnight fund is an open-ended debt scheme that invests in overnight securities, which mature in one day. Because each holding matures the next day, in general the interest rate and credit risk that come with longer maturities are low, though not nil. Each scheme's riskometer level is set out in the fund house's own documents. SEBI circular, 26 Feb 2026, CA Alley copy of the SEBI circular

6.3 · LEARN

L-015: What a fixed maturity plan is

A fixed maturity plan (FMP) is a close-ended debt scheme: investors can put money in only during its new fund offer, and the scheme runs for a set term.

For example, an FMP with a term of about three years accepts money only during its offer period and then runs until its set maturity date.

Because the term is fixed, the scheme information document sets out its maturity date and terms, and each scheme's riskometer level is in the fund house's own documents.
