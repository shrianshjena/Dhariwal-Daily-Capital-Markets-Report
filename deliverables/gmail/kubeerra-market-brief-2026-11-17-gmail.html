2.1 · WHAT IS DRIVING MARKETS

Wholesale inflation above 12%, crude near $92 and US yields above 5% frame the week before Nvidia's results

Inflation and the RBI. October wholesale price inflation rose to 12.29% from 11.39%, with fuel and power at 32.84% (Upstox News). Barclays expects a 25 basis point repo rate rise on 4 December and another in February, to 6%; economists at Emkay Global and IDFC First Bank also expect rises at both meetings, from a repo rate of 5.50% (Business Standard). Rate expectations reach bond yields, deposit rates and loan rates. Business Standard, Upstox News, PTI via The Tribune

Crude oil. Brent's January contract settled 3.55% lower at $92.44 a barrel on Monday. Bloomberg cited ample physical supply, with the IEA expecting a surplus of about 2 million barrels a day this quarter, even as attacks on tankers near the Strait of Hormuz continued (Rigzone). India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation. Bloomberg via Rigzone, Newsquawk

Foreign flows and the rupee. Exchange data show foreign investors bought a net ₹84,167 crore of Indian shares from 1 to 16 November (provisional, Kotak Neo). HDFC Sky, citing NSDL data, said November's buying so far, about $8.9 billion, is already the largest for any month on record, and linked it partly to worries about AI-linked trades overseas. PTI said the inflows helped the rupee gain 27 paise on Monday. Kotak Neo FII/DII data, HDFC Sky, PTI via HDFC Sky

Other news that matters for Indian investors

ECONOMY · MARKET-WIDE

India's October goods trade deficit widened to $39.88 billion as gold imports rose

The deficit was the widest in a year, up from $30.21 billion in September and above the $32.25 billion a Reuters poll had expected. Exports were $49.47 billion and imports $89.35 billion; gold imports rose to $5.51 billion from $1.8 billion (Reuters, released Fri 13 Nov).

WHY IT MATTERS  A wider goods deficit means more demand for dollars to pay for imports, which reaches markets through the rupee and the current account.

Sources: Reuters via MarketScreener, Business Today

 

POLICY · ON THE RADAR

SEBI set up a group led by NSE's chief to review the rules for IPOs and other share issues

On 16 Nov SEBI formed a sub-group of its Primary Market Advisory Committee, headed by NSE's Ashishkumar Chauhan, to review rules on eligibility for IPOs, QIPs and preferential issues, promoter lock-in, pricing, allotment and disclosures. Its recommendations go to the committee first; no rule has changed (Kotak Neo, PSU Watch).

WHY IT MATTERS  These rules govern how companies raise capital and what investors are told in offer documents.

6.3 · LEARN

L-014: How a fund's assets can fall while money keeps coming in

A mutual fund category's assets change for two reasons: net flows (money invested minus money withdrawn) and the change in the market value of what the schemes hold, known as mark-to-market or MTM.

In October, equity funds took net inflows of ₹32,994 crore, yet equity fund assets fell 1.1% to ₹37.15 lakh crore, which AMFI attributed to MTM losses (AMFI Monthly Note, October 2026).

So a rise or fall in a category's assets does not by itself show whether investors added or withdrew money; AMFI's monthly flow figures show that separately.
