Foreign buying. Foreign investors bought nearly $3 billion of Indian shares in the week, including $1.6 billion on Thursday, their largest one-day purchase in nearly six years, Reuters reported, as funds moved out of technology-heavy markets. Provisional exchange data put foreign net buying at ₹32,210 crore from 2 to 5 Nov and domestic institutions' net selling at ₹18,228 crore. Oil. Brent was on course for a weekly fall of about 12%, Reuters reported, after US midterm results raised hopes of de-escalation in the Iran conflict; tanker attacks in the Gulf lifted prices from two-month lows on Thursday and Friday. India imports most of its crude, so lower prices ease the import bill. Reuters via The Express Tribune

Rupee and bonds. The rupee rose 1.1% in the week to 93.905 per dollar, its strongest since 22 April and its best week in three, Reuters reported. KNOW YOUR CATEGORIES · SMALL CAP FUND

Under SEBI's categorisation rules, a small cap fund is an open-ended equity scheme that must keep at least 65% of its total assets in the shares of small cap companies, which SEBI defines as the 251st listed company onwards by full market capitalisation. SEBI's circular of 26 February 2026 kept the 65% minimum. These are equity schemes, so their value rises and falls with the share prices of smaller companies, and each scheme's riskometer level is set out in the fund house's own documents. AMFI (copy of SEBI circular of 6 Oct 2017), SEBI, Upstox News

7.3 · LEARN

L-008: What a weekly change measures

A weekly change compares one Friday's closing level with the previous Friday's close, so it shows where the week ended, not how far prices moved along the way.

This week the NIFTY 50 went from 23,053.10 on 30 Oct to 25,030.35 on 6 Nov, a change of 8.58%, though no single day rose that much.

A fund's factsheet reports returns over set periods in the same way, from one date to another; past performance may or may not be sustained in future.
