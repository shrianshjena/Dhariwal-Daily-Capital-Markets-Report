2.1 · WHAT IS DRIVING MARKETS

Rate expectations at home and abroad, lower crude and foreign buying frame the week

Rate expectations in India. Goldman Sachs projects a 25 basis point rise in the RBI's repo rate at the December meeting and a further 50 basis points in the first half of 2027, taking it from 5.50% to 6.25%; SBI Research also expects a 25 basis point rise in December (Business Standard, IANS). Rate expectations reach bond yields, deposit rates and loan rates. Business Standard, IANS via Telangana Today

Wholesale inflation. Wholesale price inflation rose to 12.29% in October from 11.39% in September, with fuel and power at 32.84%, according to provisional data released on 16 Nov; the government cited mineral oils, crude petroleum, natural gas and electricity (Kotak Neo). Input costs reach company margins and, over time, retail prices. Kotak Neo

Crude, flows and the rupee. Brent settled 1.28% lower at $91.95 on Wednesday after US crude stocks rose 3.4 million barrels in the week to 13 Nov (Reuters, The Nation). Foreign investors bought a net ₹94,229 crore of Indian shares from 1 to 18 November (provisional, Kotak Neo). Oil reaches India through the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation; flows reach large-cap demand and the currency. Reuters via Bermuda Broadcasting, The Nation (Thailand), Kotak Neo FII/DII data

2.2 · HOW GLOBAL MARKETS REACH INDIA

Fed minutes and US shares. Minutes of the Fed's 27 to 28 October meeting showed almost all officials judged further rate rises likely to be appropriate, and Reuters reported that investors expect a quarter-point rise at the 8 to 9 December meeting. US yields and the dollar. A firmer dollar weighs on the rupee and on metal prices, which reached Indian metal shares on Wednesday. Investrade, Kitco News, Closing Look

CORPORATE · SECTOR

The US FDA classified Caplin Steriles' Gummidipoondi plant as Official Action Indicated

Caplin Point Laboratories' unit Caplin Steriles received an Official Action Indicated (OAI) status, the most serious inspection outcome, for its Gummidipoondi facility, following an unannounced inspection from 13 to 21 August that ended with 10 observations; the shares fell on Wednesday (Business Standard, Angel One).

WHY IT MATTERS  An OAI status can limit what a plant may supply to the US until the issues are resolved, so it reaches the US revenue of Indian drug exporters.

KNOW YOUR CATEGORIES · MONEY MARKET FUND

AMFI's investor education page on SEBI's scheme categories describes a money market fund as a debt scheme that invests in money market instruments with a maturity of up to one year, against up to 91 days for a liquid fund and one day for an overnight fund. AMFI notes that funds holding shorter-maturity securities generally carry lower risk and lower return. Even so, such a fund can lose value, for example when rates move or an issuer defaults or is downgraded, and returns can vary. Each scheme's riskometer level is set out in the fund house's own documents. AMFI investor education page on SEBI's scheme categories

6.3 · LEARN

L-016: What a contributing SIP account is

A systematic investment plan (SIP) invests a fixed amount in a scheme at regular intervals, such as every month.

AMFI's monthly data count contributing SIP accounts: those that actually made a payment in the month. In October there were 10.00 crore of them, while the industry had 28.78 crore folios (AMFI).

A folio is an account number with one fund house; one investor can hold several folios, and one folio can hold more than one SIP, so the two counts measure different things.
