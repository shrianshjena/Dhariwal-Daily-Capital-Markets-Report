5.4 · MORE NEWS

[Policy · On the radar] SEBI is weighing limits on new margin-funded share purchases, Reuters reported
Citing two unnamed sources, Reuters reported that SEBI is considering stopping fresh margin trading facility positions in a stock once margin-funded holdings reach about 5% to 10% of its free float; the limits are not final. The margin trading book stands at ₹1.53 lakh crore, up 29% in a year, with about two-thirds in small and mid caps (Business Today, Kotak Neo). No rule has changed.
Why it matters: Margin funding is borrowed money used to buy shares, so any limit would reach leveraged demand for heavily margin-funded small and mid caps.
Source: Business Today (citing Reuters), Kotak Neo

[Economy · Sector] The government raised export levies on diesel to ₹14 a litre and on jet fuel to ₹12.50 a litre
In its fortnightly review, the Finance Ministry raised the diesel export levy from ₹11.50 to ₹14 a litre and the jet fuel (ATF) export duty from ₹11 to ₹12.50 a litre from 16 Nov; the petrol export levy stays at zero. It is the second rise in three weeks (Daily Jagran, IANS).
Why it matters: The levies set how much refiners keep on fuel exports, so they reach the margins of export-oriented refiners.
Source: Daily Jagran, IANS via Social News XYZ

6.3 · LEARN
L-015: What a fixed maturity plan is
A fixed maturity plan (FMP) is a close-ended debt scheme: investors can put money in only during its new fund offer, and the scheme runs for a set term.
For example, an FMP with a term of about three years accepts money only during its offer period and then runs until its set maturity date.
Because the term is fixed, the scheme information document sets out its maturity date and terms, and each scheme's riskometer level is in the fund house's own documents.
