Know your categories, Index Funds and ETFs: Under SEBI's categorisation circular of 26 February 2026, an index fund or exchange traded fund (ETF) is an open-ended scheme that replicates or tracks a named index, and it must keep at least 95% of its total assets in the securities of that index. Because the portfolio follows the index, its value rises and falls with that index, so its risk depends largely on what the index holds: a broad market index spreads money across many companies, while a sector or thematic index concentrates it in a few. Each scheme's riskometer level is set out in the fund house's own documents. (SEBI circular, 26 Feb 2026, SEBI)

6.3 · LEARN
L-013: What the wholesale price index measures
India's wholesale price index (WPI) tracks the prices of goods traded in bulk, grouped as primary articles, fuel and power, and manufactured products (DD India). WPI inflation is the change in that index from a year earlier.
WPI inflation was 11.39% in September, up from 9.92% in August, and the fuel and power group was 25.85% higher than a year earlier (DD India). The October reading is due today (Free Press Journal).
Because wholesale prices feed into companies' input costs, a high WPI reading can pass into consumer prices with a lag, which is one reason it is watched alongside retail inflation, which was 6.09% in October (Outlook Business).
