US election and rates: Democrats won both houses of Congress; the Nasdaq then fell 5.52% on Thursday as chip and AI shares were sold. CME FedWatch pricing put the chance of a December US rate rise at 68% on Friday, Reuters reported. (Reuters via Business Recorder, Fox Business)

Category trends:
- Among equity categories, small cap funds took in a net ₹9,038.57 crore in September, mid cap funds ₹7,834.84 crore and flexi cap funds ₹6,701.80 crore. Large cap funds drew ₹859.47 crore, while ELSS funds saw a net outflow of ₹662.14 crore. (DD India)
- Passive funds took in a net ₹11,887 crore, including equity ETFs ₹9,960 crore, gold ETFs ₹4,451 crore and silver ETFs ₹1,974 crore. (AMFI Monthly Note, September 2026)

Know your categories, Small Cap Fund: Under SEBI's categorisation rules, a small cap fund is an open-ended equity scheme that must keep at least 65% of its total assets in the shares of small cap companies, which SEBI defines as the 251st listed company onwards by full market capitalisation. SEBI's circular of 26 February 2026 kept the 65% minimum. These are equity schemes, so their value rises and falls with the share prices of smaller companies, and each scheme's riskometer level is set out in the fund house's own documents. (AMFI (copy of SEBI circular of 6 Oct 2017), SEBI, Upstox News)

7.3 · LEARN
L-008: What a weekly change measures
A weekly change compares one Friday's closing level with the previous Friday's close, so it shows where the week ended, not how far prices moved along the way.
This week the NIFTY 50 went from 23,053.10 on 30 Oct to 25,030.35 on 6 Nov, a change of 8.58%, though no single day rose that much.
A fund's factsheet reports returns over set periods in the same way, from one date to another; past performance may or may not be sustained in future.
