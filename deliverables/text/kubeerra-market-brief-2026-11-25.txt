6.3 · LEARN
L-019: What a 1:1 bonus issue changes, and what it does not
A bonus issue gives existing shareholders extra shares free, in proportion to what they hold. The company funds it by moving money from its free reserves or securities premium into share capital, so no cash leaves the company (Upstox learning centre).
In a 1:1 bonus, each share held on the record date earns one more, so the number of shares doubles. The share price adjusts on the ex-bonus date in the same proportion, so the total value of a holding is unchanged on that day: 100 shares at ₹500 become 200 shares at ₹250 (Upstox learning centre).
Tech Mahindra has set 30 Nov as the record date for its 1:1 bonus, subject to shareholder approval, with the new shares tradable from 2 Dec (Upstox News). A bonus changes the count of shares, not the value of the business.
