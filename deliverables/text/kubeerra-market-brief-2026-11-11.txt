2.1 · WHAT IS DRIVING MARKETS
US inflation, crude and India's own price data frame the first session after the holiday
- Crude oil: Brent settled at $91.49 on Tuesday, its lowest since 31 Aug, after President Trump said he would put off new strikes on Iran and Middle East shipments neared prewar levels (Reuters, Rigzone). India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation. (Reuters via Investing.com, Rigzone)
- Foreign flows: Foreign investors bought a net ₹53,172 crore in the cash market from 1 to 9 Nov while domestic institutions sold ₹32,069 crore (provisional, Kotak Neo). Foreign flows feed into large-cap share prices and the rupee; provisional figures can be revised. (Kotak Neo FII/DII data, Trendlyne)
- Inflation in India: Retail inflation was 5.54% in September (provisional), with food prices up 7.74% (Business Standard). ICRA's Aditi Nayar expects October's reading at 6.1% (NewsBytes). Inflation readings shape rate expectations, which reach bond yields and loan rates. (Business Standard, NewsBytes)
- The rupee and bonds: The rupee firmed 5 paise on Monday on easing crude and foreign inflows, PTI reported, while the 10-year yield rose to 7.17% as investors took profits after a rally (Trading Economics). Bond prices rise when yields fall. (PTI via Telangana Today, Trading Economics)

2.2 · HOW GLOBAL MARKETS REACH INDIA
- US inflation and the Fed: US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October and core prices 0.3% (BLS). After the data, CME FedWatch showed a December Fed rate rise fully priced (Seoul Economic Daily), and Fed Governor Waller said he would back at least a quarter-point rise (Reuters). Higher US rates can weigh on foreign flows into Indian shares and bonds. (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, Seoul Economic Daily, Reuters via Investing.com)
- US yields and the dollar: The 10-year Treasury yield rose about 10 basis points to near 5% on Tuesday and the dollar index edged higher (Trading Economics). A firmer dollar tends to weigh on the rupee and other emerging-market currencies. (Trading Economics, CappNotes)
- Asian markets: On Tuesday, while India was shut, Japan's Nikkei rose 2.9% and Korea's Kospi 4.4% (AP). On Wednesday morning the Kospi opened lower, then turned higher, Aju Press reported. (AP via KSAT, Aju Press)

- Investors added about 27.13 lakh folios in September, taking the total to 28.62 crore. (AMFI Monthly Note, September 2026)

Know your categories, Multi Cap Fund: Under SEBI's categorisation rules, a multi cap fund must keep at least 75% of its assets in equity and equity-related instruments, with at least 25% each in large cap, mid cap and small cap companies. With at least a quarter in each size band, a fixed part of the portfolio always sits in mid and small companies, whose share prices tend to swing more than those of large companies. Each scheme's riskometer level is set out in the fund house's own documents. (SEBI, SEBI circular text (mirror))

- Several fund houses, 6 to 7 Nov: HDFC, ICICI Prudential, Aditya Birla Sun Life and Franklin Templeton mutual funds revised the riskometer levels of some schemes, most based on their end-October portfolios, Upstox News reported; some levels went up and some down. (Upstox News)

6.3 · LEARN
L-010: Why a scheme's riskometer can change
Every mutual fund scheme shows a riskometer, a dial that places its risk on a scale; the levels named in last week's revisions ran from Low to Very High.
The level follows what the scheme holds. On 6 and 7 November several fund houses revised levels, most based on their end-October portfolios, moving some schemes up and some down (Upstox News).
A change in level does not mean a scheme has changed its category or objective. The latest level, and the reasons for it, are in the fund house's own documents.
