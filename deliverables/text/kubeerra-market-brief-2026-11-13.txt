6.3 · LEARN
L-012: What the retail inflation number measures
India's monthly retail inflation figure measures how much the prices of a basket of goods and services that households buy have changed from a year earlier.
In October it was 6.09%, up from 5.54% in September, and consumer food inflation was 8.94% (Reuters, Business Today). Food prices can move the figure noticeably: onion prices were up 116.22% from a year earlier (Business Today).
The RBI's tolerance band has an upper limit of 6%, so a reading above it feeds into expectations for the policy rate, which economists at SBI Research and Bank of Baroda have discussed ahead of the RBI's 2 to 4 December review (Business Today, Hello Banker).
