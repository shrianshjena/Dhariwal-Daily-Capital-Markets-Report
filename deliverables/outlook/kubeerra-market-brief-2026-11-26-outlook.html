KNOW YOUR CATEGORIES · DYNAMIC ASSET ALLOCATION OR BALANCED ADVANTAGE

Under SEBI's scheme categories, as revised in February 2026 and reported by Upstox, a dynamic asset allocation or balanced advantage fund is an open-ended hybrid scheme that invests in equity and debt and manages the split between them dynamically. Because that split changes over time, such a fund carries share-price risk on its equity part and interest-rate and credit risk on its debt part, in proportions that vary. Each scheme's riskometer level and risk factors are in the fund house's own documents. Upstox News, SEBI circular page

6.3 · LEARN

L-020: What a riskometer level tells you

SEBI's riskometer places every mutual fund scheme at one of six levels: Low, Low to Moderate, Moderate, Moderately High, High and Very High (SEBI circular, October 2020).

The fund house sets the level from the risk of the securities the scheme holds and reviews it every month, publishing it with the portfolio on its own website and AMFI's; if the level changes, unitholders are told (SEBI). For shares the assessment looks at factors such as market size, volatility and liquidity, and for bonds at credit, interest-rate and liquidity risk (Mutual Funds Sahi Hai).

The level appears in the scheme information document, the key information memorandum and the application form, next to the scheme's name (SEBI). It describes the risk of what a scheme holds today; it is not a measure of returns, and it can change as the portfolio changes.
