2.1 · WHAT IS DRIVING MARKETS

Inflation above 6%, crude near $96 and US yields above 5% frame Friday's session

Inflation and the RBI. October retail inflation rose to 6.09% from 5.54%, with consumer food inflation at 8.94% (Reuters, Business Today). SBI Research now expects a 25 basis point rise at the RBI's 2 to 4 December review rather than 50, and Bank of Baroda's Madan Sabnavis expects 25 or 50 (Business Today). Rate expectations reach bond yields, deposit rates and loan rates. Business Today, Reuters via Investing.com, The Hans India

Crude oil. Brent settled 3.3% higher at $95.82 on Thursday after Yemen's Houthis claimed attacks on Saudi energy sites, having fallen about $1 in early Asian trade (Reuters). India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation. The Nation (citing Reuters), Reuters via Investing.com

The rupee and bonds. The rupee closed 33 paise weaker at a provisional 94.04 per dollar as the dollar firmed (PTI), and the 10-year G-Sec yield was about 7.25%, up 4 basis points (Trading Economics). A weaker rupee raises the cost of imports; bond prices fall when yields rise. PTI via HDFC Sky, Trading Economics

Foreign flows and index changes. Foreign investors bought a net ₹73,566 crore in the cash market from 1 to 12 Nov (provisional, Kotak Neo). Separately, Nuvama estimates about $2 billion of passive inflows from MSCI's November index review, effective 30 Nov (Business Standard). Foreign flows feed into large-cap prices and the rupee. Kotak Neo FII/DII data, Business Standard

 

2.2 · HOW GLOBAL MARKETS REACH INDIA

US shares. The S&P 500, Dow and Nasdaq each fell about 0.4% on Thursday and the Russell 2000 fell 1.7% (AP). Moves on Wall Street tend to shape foreign investors' appetite for emerging market shares, including India's. AP via Yahoo Finance

US yields, the Fed and the dollar. The US 10-year yield rose back above 5% to about 5.10% and the dollar index to 103.19 (CappNotes). CME FedWatch put the chance of a quarter-point Fed rise in December near 85% (Investing.com). Higher US yields and a firmer dollar tend to weigh on the rupee and on foreign flows into Indian bonds and shares. CappNotes, Investing.com, Kitco

KNOW YOUR CATEGORIES · ELSS

Under SEBI's categorisation rules, an ELSS (equity linked savings scheme, often called a tax saver fund) must keep at least 80% of its assets in equity and equity-related instruments, and it follows the Equity Linked Saving Scheme, 2005, notified by the Ministry of Finance. With at least four-fifths of the portfolio in shares, its value can rise and fall with the stock market. Each scheme's riskometer level is set out in the fund house's own documents. SEBI, SEBI circular (copy hosted by CAalley)

6.3 · LEARN

L-012: What the retail inflation number measures

India's monthly retail inflation figure measures how much the prices of a basket of goods and services that households buy have changed from a year earlier.

In October it was 6.09%, up from 5.54% in September, and consumer food inflation was 8.94% (Reuters, Business Today). Food prices can move the figure noticeably: onion prices were up 116.22% from a year earlier (Business Today).

The RBI's tolerance band has an upper limit of 6%, so a reading above it feeds into expectations for the policy rate, which economists at SBI Research and Bank of Baroda have discussed ahead of the RBI's 2 to 4 December review (Business Today, Hello Banker).
