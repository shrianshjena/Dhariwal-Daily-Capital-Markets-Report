2.1 · WHAT IS DRIVING MARKETS

India's inflation data, US rates and crude frame Thursday's session

Inflation in India. October retail inflation is due today. ICRA's Aditi Nayar expects 6.1% (NewsBytes) and Bank of Baroda's economists about 6%, citing food prices (Devdiscourse); September's reading was 5.54%. Inflation readings shape rate expectations, which reach bond yields and loan rates. NewsBytes, Devdiscourse

Foreign flows. Foreign investors bought a net ₹65,735 crore in the cash market from 1 to 11 Nov while domestic institutions sold ₹40,504 crore (provisional, Kotak Neo). Foreign flows feed into large-cap share prices and the rupee; provisional figures can be revised. Kotak Neo FII/DII data, Trendlyne

Crude oil. Brent settled at $92.76 on Wednesday, up $1.27, after Iran said its enriched nuclear material would stay in the country (Reuters); it had fallen to a two-month low on Tuesday. India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation. EconoTimes (citing Reuters), SBS News

The rupee and bonds. The rupee firmed 6 paise to 93.71 on Wednesday on foreign inflows and crude near a two-month low during Indian trading (PTI); Brent rose later in the day. The 10-year yield rose 6 basis points to 7.21% as US Treasury yields climbed, with a ₹34,000 crore government bond auction due on Friday (Business Standard, Reuters). Bond prices fall when yields rise. Business Standard, PTI via The Hans India, Reuters via Business Recorder

 

2.2 · HOW GLOBAL MARKETS REACH INDIA

US inflation and the Fed. After October's US consumer prices rose 0.4% against the 0.3% forecast cited by Reuters, markets fully priced a quarter-point Fed rate rise in December; Goldman Sachs and Deutsche Bank now expect rises starting in December (Reuters). Higher US rates can weigh on foreign flows into Indian shares and bonds. Reuters via Euronext

US yields and the dollar. The US cash Treasury market was shut on Wednesday for Veterans Day; the 10-year yield had closed at 4.98% on Tuesday (CappNotes). The dollar firmed on Wednesday (Reuters), and a firmer dollar tends to weigh on the rupee and other emerging-market currencies. CappNotes, Reuters via Euronext, Kiplinger

Asian markets. On Wednesday, Japan's Nikkei rose 1.3% (AP). On Thursday morning Korea's Kospi opened slightly higher, then moved between small gains and losses (Aju Press). AP via KSAT, Aju Press

KNOW YOUR CATEGORIES · LARGE & MID CAP FUND

Under SEBI's categorisation rules, a large and mid cap fund must keep at least 35% of its assets in large cap companies and at least 35% in mid cap companies. Large caps are the 100 largest companies by full market value and mid caps those ranked 101 to 250, so at least about a third of the portfolio always sits in mid-sized companies, whose share prices have tended to swing more than those of the largest companies. Each scheme's riskometer level is set out in the fund house's own documents. SEBI, Outlook Money

6.3 · LEARN

L-011: Why total fund flows can jump while equity flows dip

AMFI's monthly data reports net flows for each category, and the total adds them all up, including money parked for days or weeks.

In October, equity funds took in ₹32,994 crore, less than in September, yet total net inflows were about ₹1.74 lakh crore, because liquid and overnight funds alone took about ₹1.25 lakh crore (AMFI). Companies and institutions often use these funds for short-term cash.

So a large total does not by itself say where long-term investors are putting money; the category lines in AMFI's note show that.
