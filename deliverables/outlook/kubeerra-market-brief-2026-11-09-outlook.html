2.1 · WHAT IS DRIVING MARKETS

Oil, foreign flows and two inflation readings frame a holiday-shortened week

Crude oil. Brent fell more than 13% last week after the US midterm results, The National reported. Bloomberg put the drop above 16% as investors weighed the chance of US and Iran talks, and cited Rystad Energy's Jorge Leon saying US pressure does not mean a deal is close. India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation. The National, Bloomberg via Bloomingbit

Foreign flows. Foreign investors were net buyers of ₹45,960 crore in the cash market from 2 to 6 Nov while domestic institutions sold ₹24,197 crore (provisional, Upstox). Foreign flows feed into large-cap share prices and the rupee; provisional figures can be revised. Upstox FII/DII data, PTI via HDFC Sky

Inflation and the RBI. Eleven economists polled by Financial Express expect October retail inflation at 6.1%, against 5.54% in September; ICRA's Aditi Nayar also expects 6.1%. The RBI raised the repo rate to 5.50% in October (Business Standard). Inflation readings shape rate expectations, which reach bond yields and loan rates. Financial Express poll via IPA Newspack, Moody's via TradingView, Business Standard

The rupee and bonds. The rupee firmed 52 paise on Friday on a softer dollar, lower crude and buoyant shares, PTI reported. The 10-year yield was 7.19% (Business Standard). Bond prices rise when yields fall. PTI via HDFC Sky, Business Standard

 

2.2 · HOW GLOBAL MARKETS REACH INDIA

US yields and the Fed. The US 10-year yield ended Friday near 4.86% after strong October payrolls. LSEG data put the chance of a quarter-point Fed rate rise on 9 Dec at 75%, Dow Jones reported. Higher US yields can weigh on foreign flows into Indian shares and bonds. Dow Jones via MarketScreener, JournalArta

The dollar. The dollar index rose about 0.7% to 102.3 on Friday after the jobs data (JournalArta, Investrade). A firmer dollar tends to weigh on the rupee and other emerging-market currencies. JournalArta, Investrade

Technology shares. The Nasdaq rose 1.09% on Friday after Thursday's sell-off but ended the week lower. Moves in global technology shares reach Indian IT companies and foreign allocations. Investrade, JournalArta

KNOW YOUR CATEGORIES · FLEXI CAP FUND

Under SEBI's categorisation rules, a flexi cap fund must invest at least 65% of its assets in equity and equity-related instruments, with no minimum or maximum for large, mid or small cap companies. Because the manager can change the mix across company sizes, the scheme's risk can change with its holdings, and each scheme's riskometer level is set out in the fund house's own documents. These are equity schemes, so their value rises and falls with share prices. SEBI, SEBI circular text (mirror)

6.3 · LEARN

L-009: What Muhurat trading is

Muhurat trading is a short special session that NSE and BSE hold on Diwali to mark the start of the new Samvat year in the Hindu calendar.

This year's session ran for a little over an hour on the afternoon of Sunday 8 November, opening Samvat 2083 (PTI). Trades in it create settlement obligations, as on a normal day (Kotak Neo).

Because the session lasts about an hour, it reflects far less trading than a full day; index levels over longer periods give a fuller picture of the market.
