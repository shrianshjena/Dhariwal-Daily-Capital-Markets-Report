The NIFTY 50 rose 0.63% to 23,960.60 and the Sensex 0.50% to 77,301.40 on Wednesday, a fifth straight gain. Smaller companies did better: the NIFTY Smallcap 100 rose 2.00% and the Midcap 100 1.55%.
 
Media (up 5.15%), realty (up 2.85%) and metal (up 2.80%) shares led, while Nifty IT, down 1.50%, was the only sector index lower.

2.1 · WHAT IS DRIVING MARKETS

Oil's sharp fall and foreign buying set the tone; Diwali week brings a special session and a holiday

Crude oil. Brent settled at $94.62 a barrel on 4 Nov, down 8.7% in a day, as investors weighed whether the US midterm results bring an earlier end to the Iran war, Reuters reported. India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee, fuel costs and inflation. Reuters via Cyprus Mail, Seoul Economic Daily

Foreign flows. Foreign institutions bought a net ₹7,284 crore on 4 Nov (provisional), after ₹6,837 crore on 3 Nov. Foreign flows feed into large-cap share prices and the rupee; provisional figures can be revised when final data is published. Trendlyne (exchange data), Upstox FII/DII data

The rupee. The rupee rose 17 paise to 94.58 per dollar (PTI, provisional). Reuters reported a two-week high early in the day, with gains trimmed by importer dollar demand, and named a weaker dollar and lower oil after the midterms as the drivers. Reuters via Business Recorder, PTI via HDFC Sky

IT shares. Nifty IT fell 1.50% on a day the wider market rose, with TCS and HCL Technologies the largest NIFTY 50 decliners. Trading Economics attributed the falls to profit-taking amid concerns about global technology demand. Trading Economics, Upstox News

 

2.2 · HOW GLOBAL MARKETS REACH INDIA

US shares. The Dow rose 3.03%, the S&P 500 1.58% and the Nasdaq Composite 1.44% on 4 Nov after Democrats won both houses of Congress. US risk appetite reaches India through foreign portfolio flows. StockTitan (citing CNBC), Reuters via The Standard

US yields and the dollar. The US 10-year Treasury yield fell about 6 basis points to about 4.89%, and the dollar index fell about 0.3%. A softer dollar and lower US yields bear on flows into Indian shares and bonds and on the rupee. The Motley Fool, Reuters via Euronext

US technology. AI data centre stocks fell on 4 Nov while large platforms such as Amazon, Meta and Alphabet rose, Reuters reported. US technology spending is the main demand channel for Indian IT services companies. Reuters via The Standard, Finwire

KNOW YOUR CATEGORIES · LARGE CAP FUND

Under SEBI's categorisation rules, a large cap fund is an open-ended equity scheme that must keep at least 80% of its total assets in the shares of large cap companies, which SEBI defines as the 1st to 100th listed companies by full market capitalisation. SEBI's circular of 26 February 2026 kept this 80% floor. Because these are equity schemes, their value rises and falls with the share prices of the largest listed companies: a focus on large companies does not remove market risk, and each scheme's riskometer level is set out in the fund house's own documents. SEBI, AMFI (copy of SEBI circular of 6 Oct 2017), CA Alley (copy of SEBI circular of 26 Feb 2026)

6.3 · LEARN

L-006: Why liquid funds see large withdrawals around tax dates

Liquid funds invest in very short-term debt, such as treasury bills and commercial paper, and companies often use them to park cash they will need within weeks.

When an advance tax deadline approaches, many companies withdraw that cash to pay their tax. In September, AMFI linked the debt fund outflows to these tax-related cash needs, and liquid funds alone saw a net outflow of ₹95,743 crore.

Large outflows like these say more about the calendar than about any one scheme. A liquid fund still carries interest rate and credit risk, which the scheme's riskometer and documents describe.
