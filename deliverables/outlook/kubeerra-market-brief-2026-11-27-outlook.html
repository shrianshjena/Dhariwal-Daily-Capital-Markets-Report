KNOW YOUR CATEGORIES · MULTI ASSET ALLOCATION

Under SEBI's scheme categories, as revised in February 2026 and summarised by Upstox, a multi asset allocation fund is a hybrid scheme that invests in at least three asset classes, such as equity, debt and gold or other commodities, with at least 10% in each. Because it holds assets that can move differently, its risk depends on how much it holds in each, and its value can still fall when share or commodity prices fall. Each scheme's riskometer level and risk factors are in the fund house's own documents. Upstox News, SEBI circular page, SEBI circular, 2017 (hosted by AMFI)

6.3 · LEARN

L-021: Why fund assets can fall in a month when money flows in

A fund category's assets under management (AUM) change for two reasons: the money investors put in or take out (net flows), and the change in the market value of what the funds already hold, known as mark-to-market.

In October 2026, equity funds took in a net ₹32,994 crore, yet their assets fell 1.1% to ₹37.15 lakh crore; AMFI attributed the fall to mark-to-market losses (AMFI Monthly Note, October 2026). The fall in the value of holdings was larger than the new money that came in.

The reverse can also happen: assets can rise in a month of net outflows if prices rise enough. So a change in AUM does not on its own show whether investors added money, and AMFI's monthly note reports net flows and assets as separate figures.
