2.1 · WHAT IS DRIVING MARKETS

Wholesale prices and trade data today; inflation above 6%, crude near $96 and US yields near 5.15% frame the week

Inflation and the RBI. October retail inflation rose to 6.09% from 5.54%, above the RBI's 6% upper tolerance limit, with food inflation at 8.94% (PTI, Outlook Business). Radhika Rao of DBS Bank expects another rate rise in December, and ICRA's Aditi Nayar said a 25 basis point rise at the 2 to 4 December meeting appears almost certain (Outlook Business). Rate expectations reach bond yields, deposit rates and loan rates. Outlook Business, PTI via DT Next

Wholesale prices. Wholesale price inflation was 11.39% in September, up from 9.92% in August, with fuel and power at 25.85% (DD India); the October figure is due today. Wholesale prices feed into companies' input costs and, with a lag, into consumer prices. DD India, Free Press Journal

Crude oil. Brent settled at $95.84 on Friday after a second weekly fall. Bloomberg pointed to more Middle East supply and an International Energy Agency forecast of a surplus this quarter, against continued attacks on shipping in the Gulf region (Rigzone). India imports most of its crude, so oil prices reach the import bill, the rupee and fuel inflation. Bloomberg via Rigzone, Gate (citing Reuters data)

Foreign flows. Exchange data show foreign investors bought a net ₹79,302 crore of Indian shares from 1 to 13 November (provisional, Kotak Neo). Geojit's VK Vijayakumar linked the buying to money moving away from AI-linked markets (IANS). Foreign buying adds demand for large-cap shares and for rupees. Kotak Neo FII/DII data, IANS via The Hans India

KNOW YOUR CATEGORIES · INDEX FUNDS AND ETFS

Under SEBI's categorisation circular of 26 February 2026, an index fund or exchange traded fund (ETF) is an open-ended scheme that replicates or tracks a named index, and it must keep at least 95% of its total assets in the securities of that index. Because the portfolio follows the index, its value rises and falls with that index, so its risk depends largely on what the index holds: a broad market index spreads money across many companies, while a sector or thematic index concentrates it in a few. Each scheme's riskometer level is set out in the fund house's own documents. SEBI circular, 26 Feb 2026, SEBI

6.3 · LEARN

L-013: What the wholesale price index measures

India's wholesale price index (WPI) tracks the prices of goods traded in bulk, grouped as primary articles, fuel and power, and manufactured products (DD India). WPI inflation is the change in that index from a year earlier.

WPI inflation was 11.39% in September, up from 9.92% in August, and the fuel and power group was 25.85% higher than a year earlier (DD India). The October reading is due today (Free Press Journal).

Because wholesale prices feed into companies' input costs, a high WPI reading can pass into consumer prices with a lag, which is one reason it is watched alongside retail inflation, which was 6.09% in October (Outlook Business).
