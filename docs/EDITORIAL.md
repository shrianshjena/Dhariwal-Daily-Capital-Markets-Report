# Editorial standards

Every story answers two questions in Kubeerra's voice: what happened, and why it matters to an
Indian investor. Plain words, a dated and sourced number, no prediction.

## Issue shape (morning edition, from 5 Nov 2026, D-046)

At a glance (six key numbers, three to five bullets) · In this issue (contents) ·
1 Markets: snapshot (with NIFTY Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100), top gainers and losers, technical
snapshot, sectors, flows · 2 Fundamental outlook: what is driving markets, global cues for
India, events and data ahead · 3 Global markets: the US session, world markets, global events ·
4 Gold and silver · 5 Companies: results, big movers, corporate actions, IPO desk, more news ·
6 Mutual funds: corner, talk to us, learn · 7 Calendar · Important disclosures (a full page in
the PDF). Sections without data drop out and the numbering closes up.

Say each fact once, in the section it belongs to. The mutual fund corner's headline starts with
the latest month's facts ("In September, ..."), never with what is not yet published.

The fundamental outlook explains what is moving markets and what is scheduled; any expectation
belongs to a named source ("economists polled by Reuters expect ..."). The technical snapshot
gives numbers, never a direction (no bullish, bearish, support, resistance or breakout).

Section headlines state the finding in sentence case ("Indices ended lower as banks weighed"),
never a label ("Market update").

## Story pattern

1. Tags: a topic (Markets, Flows, Economy, Global, IPO, Mutual funds, Corporate, Policy) and a
   relevance level with fixed meaning:
   - Market-wide: moves the broad indices or institutional flows.
   - Sector: affects one sector or asset class.
   - On the radar: worth knowing, no immediate effect.
2. Headline: one complete thought, sentence case, no truncation, no ALL CAPS.
3. What happened: one or two sentences with figures, units and dates.
4. Why it matters: one or two sentences on the transmission channel to Indian investors
   (crude to the import bill to inflation; flows to the rupee). No forecast, no call.
5. Source: publication name with a link.

## Voice

Calm, specific, plain. Explain jargon at first use. Describe moves with numbers, never with
crash, bloodbath, panic, frenzy, soar or plunge. When India lags global markets, give context
rather than alarm; never cheerlead either. Every item must pass an India-relevance test.

## Numbers

- Indian system: ₹4,240 crore, ₹1.2 lakh crore, ₹1,42,484. A true minus sign (−) for negatives.
- Index moves to two decimals. Every number carries its unit and an as-of date or time.
- FII/DII figures are provisional until NSDL publishes; say so.

## Never (the distributor lane)

Buy, sell or hold language; price targets; bullish or bearish views on a company; re-rating
talk; opinions on IPO applications; grey market premium; scheme rankings by returns or "best funds";
return projections beyond the 12% illustration policy; testimonials; describing Kubeerra as
an adviser, advisory, wealth manager, research provider or portfolio manager.

## Sources and copyright

Write every summary in our own words and name the source (D-039: readers' links go only to
kubeerra.com blog posts and service pages, which may be reused). No copied paragraphs, headlines or
images. A quote, if one is essential, stays under 15 words. No content from unregistered
finfluencers.

## Style

No em dashes or en dashes: use commas, colons, parentheses or "to". Dates as 29 Oct 2026;
times as 3:30 PM IST.
